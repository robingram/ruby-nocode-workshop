# Tool comparison

| | Lovable | Knack | Base44 | Softr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Build style** | Prompt | Visual (database-first) | Prompt | Visual (blocks) |
| **Code access** | Full code, exportable | None | Partial | None |
| **Learning curve** | Low to start, higher to fix | Medium–high | Low | Low |
| **Flexibility** | Very high | Medium | High | Low–medium |
| **Free option** | 5 credits/day | 14-day trial | 5 messages/day | Free forever |
| **Best for** | Custom apps and prototypes | Data-heavy business apps | Quick full-stack tools | Portals and internal tools |

**Takeaway:** prompt-first tools trade control for speed; visual builders trade flexibility for predictability.
